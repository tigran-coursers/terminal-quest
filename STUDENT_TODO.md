- [ ] Complete [integration tasks](03-integration/TODO.md): [SSH](03-integration/ssh/TODO.md), [SCP](03-integration/file-transfer/TODO.md), and [Java deployment](03-integration/java-deployment/TODO.md).
- [ ] Complete all six starter scripts in [scripts](scripts).

- [ ] Submit the report, scripts, and evidence; exclude keys, passwords, JARs, logs, and PIDs.
